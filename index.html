YT Playlist Builder

A personal tool for keeping one YouTube playlist tidy.

YT Playlist Builder is a small, single-user program that runs on its owner's own computer. It watches a handful of YouTube channels, adds their new uploads to a private playlist, and emails a nightly summary of what it added. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than its author.

What it does

  • Checks a fixed list of YouTube channels once a day for new uploads.
  • Adds matching uploads to one playlist owned by the person running it.
  • Moves items older than three months into an archive playlist.
  • Emails the owner a short summary of what was added.

Why it needs access to YouTube

Reading public YouTube data needs no permission. Writing to a playlist does, so the program asks for the youtube.force-ssl scope in order to create playlists and add or remove items in the account it is run from. That is the only thing it uses the permission for.
